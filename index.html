About CLC Cure Life Care

CLC Cure Life Care — printed on pack as CLC curelifecare — is an Indian wellness brand for everyday family routines. The catalogue is small on purpose: one bottle for feeding, one oil for hair, one wash for the face.

The brand is marketed from Rai, Chhattisgarh. Finished goods are made in India. Glowryl is manufactured by Zole Biotech Pvt. Ltd., an ISO 9001:2015 and WHO-GMP certified unit in Mohali, Punjab.
